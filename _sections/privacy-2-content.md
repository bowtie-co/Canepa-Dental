### Our Commitment to Your Privacy
This Notice describes how Canepa Dental may use and disclose your protected health information (PHI) in accordance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and applicable Washington State law. We are required by law to:
- Maintain the privacy of your protected health information
- Provide you with this Notice of our legal duties and privacy practices
- Follow the terms of the Notice currently in effect

### How We May Use and Disclose Your Health Information
We may use and disclose your health information without your written authorization for the following purposes:
  1. Treatment
  We may use and disclose your health information to provide, coordinate, or manage your dental care. For example, we may share information with specialists, laboratories, pharmacies, or other health care providers involved in your treatment.
  2. Payment
  We may use and disclose your health information to obtain payment for services provided, including billing you, your insurance company, or a third party.
  3. Health Care Operations
  We may use and disclose your health information for practice operations such as quality assessment, staff training, licensing, and accreditation activities.
  4. As Required by Law
  We may disclose your health information when required by federal, state, or local law.
  5. Public Health and Safety
  We may disclose your information for public health activities, to prevent serious threats to health or safety, or to report suspected abuse or neglect, as permitted by law.

### Special Protections for Substance Use Disorder Treatment Records (42 CFR Part 2)
Our dental practice is not a federally assisted Substance Use Disorder (SUD) treatment program. However, we may receive health information about you from a provider or program that is subject to a federal confidentiality law known as 42 Code of Federal Regulations (CFR) Part 2.
If we receive records protected under 42 CFR Part 2:
These records are subject to stricter privacy protections than other medical information protected under HIPAA.
We will not use or disclose these records without your written authorization, except as permitted by federal law.
Disclosures may be permitted in limited circumstances such as medical emergencies, certain public health reporting, audits, research, or court orders that meet federal requirements.
Prohibition on Redisclosure
Federal law prohibits the recipient of SUD treatment records from redisclosing this information unless further disclosure is expressly permitted by your written consent or otherwise allowed by 42 CFR Part 2.
Any disclosure made with your written consent will include a statement notifying the recipient of this restriction.

### Uses and Disclosures Requiring Your Written Authorization
We will obtain your written authorization before:
- Disclosing psychotherapy notes (if applicable)
- Using your information for marketing purposes (if required by law)
- Selling your protected health information
- Disclosing SUD treatment records protected under 42 CFR Part 2, except as otherwise permitted by law
You may revoke your authorization in writing at any time, except to the extent we have already acted on it.

### Your Rights Regarding Your Health Information
You have the right to:
  1. Inspect and Obtain a Copy
  Request access to your health records, subject to certain limitations.
  2. Request an Amendment
  Request correction of your health information if you believe it is inaccurate or incomplete.
  3. Request an Accounting of Disclosures
  Receive a list of certain disclosures we have made of your health information.
  4. Request Restrictions
  Request restrictions on certain uses and disclosures of your information. We are not required to agree to all requested restrictions, except where required by law.
  5. Request Confidential Communications
  Request that we communicate with you in a specific way (for example, at a specific phone number or address).
  6. Receive a Paper Copy of This Notice
  You may request a paper copy of this Notice at any time.


### Breach Notification
If a breach occurs that compromises the privacy or security of your protected health information, we will notify you as required by law.
